## Me

Escéptica, skeptical, skeptisch o Skeksis  ¿?

I speak Spanish, English, and German—and sometimes all three make it into the same sentence. It’s not a bug; it’s a feature with a questionable release process.

I’m a software engineer in Berlin. I build things, learn things, and try not to confuse “reproducible” with “under control.” When I’m away from a terminal, I’m usually still curious about something—often a language, occasionally a guitar, and sometimes why I walked into the room.

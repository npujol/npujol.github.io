## Fullscreen Content

Fullscreen pages display content without any header, footer, or navigation elements. They occupy the entire viewport.

### Creating Fullscreen Pages

Use `type: 'fullscreen'` in your frontmatter:

```toml
---
type: 'fullscreen'
title: 'Fullscreen Page Title'
---

Your content here.
```

### Use Cases

Fullscreen pages are ideal for:

- **Presentations** — Display content without distractions
- **Showcases** — Highlight specific work or projects
- **Articles** — Focus readers on the content
- **Gallery pages** — Display images without UI elements

### Fullscreen Example

A fullscreen page can contain any valid Markdown or HTML content. The theme ensures the content is properly centered and readable.

---

*Fullscreen pages are accessible via direct links or from slides with `target` parameters.*
